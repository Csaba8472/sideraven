Sideraven 1.1 makes the Sidebar yours: your Spaces' colours, your buttons, your layout, and a full-screen mode for when you want nothing but the page.

## New features

- **Space themes.** Each Space tints the Sidebar, its Essentials and address bar, and the frame around the page with its colours. Spaces synced from Zen bring their desktop gradient; Spaces made on your device use their accent. Look and feel → Theme.
- **Pure black.** A black dark theme for OLED screens. Your Space colours stay as accents.
- **Full screen.** Hide Android's status bar, or both it and the navigation bar. Swipe in from the top or bottom edge to see them for a moment. Off unless you turn it on: Look and feel → Full screen.
- **Swipe the address bar.** Swipe the Sidebar's address bar up for a new tab, or sideways for the next or previous tab.
- **Tabs from the bottom.** The Tab List can rest on the bottom edge and grow upwards, so your newest tabs sit by the address bar, within thumb reach. Tabs → Tabs start from.
- **Synced tabs.** Your tabs from other devices are a tap away, from a Sidebar button or the ⋮ menu.
- **Choose your Sidebar buttons.** Synced tabs, Change mode and Move address bar can each sit in the Sidebar or move to the ⋮ menu.
- **Arrange sidebar.** In Collapsed mode, pick Arrange sidebar from the ⋮ menu and drag the bars under Essentials and Pinned Tabs to size them. Portrait and landscape keep their own sizes, and Reset goes back to automatic.
- **Collapsed mode on phones.** Keep a narrow strip of icons on screen instead of a hidden Sidebar.

## Changes

- **New look for Sideraven Settings.** Switches for on and off, buttons for short choices, pictures for the Sidebar's mode and the address bar's layout, and plainer wording throughout. Essentials settings moved into Tabs.
- **Zen's names.** The modes are now Expanded, Collapsed and Compact (1.0's Persistent and Auto-Hide), and the address bar layouts are Sidebar and toolbar, or Only sidebar.
- **The address bar starts in the Sidebar** on new installs, and for anyone who never chose a layout. If you picked one, it stays.
- In Collapsed mode, Essentials and Pinned Tabs size themselves, and the Tab List always keeps at least 40% of the height. This replaces 1.0's draggable dividers.
- Switching to a Space with no tabs, or making a new one, shows its home page instead of opening the keyboard.
- Swiping Fenix's toolbar up no longer opens the tab tray beside a Sidebar: your tabs are already in it. (It worked on web pages but not on the home page.)
- The first-run screen for choosing the toolbar's position shows the raven instead of the fox.

## Fixes

- Fixed two address bars appearing after "Open in Sideraven" from another app's tab.
- Fixed a keyboard with nowhere to type when switching to private (or back) with no tabs open; the search pop-up opens instead.
- Fixed the private home page showing a lighter block of purple behind its description.
- Fixed the bar under Pinned Tabs lagging behind your finger while arranging.
